Alalarm Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Alalarm is made by Vercodex.

The short version

Your alarms stay on your phone. Vercodex does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show them. Alalarm Pro removes the ads.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms, their history (rang, done, snoozed) and your settings are saved only on your device. Vercodex has no server for Alalarm and never receives them. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on that IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. Your alarms, notes and history are never sent with ad requests.

Where the law requires it, Alalarm asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change that choice in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Buying Alalarm Pro removes ads completely.

Purchases

Alalarm Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. They process the payment under their own privacy policies. Alalarm only receives confirmation from the store, on your device, that you own Pro. We never see your payment details.

Children

Alalarm can be used by people of any age. Vercodex collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are requested as non-personalized and child-directed, so AdMob does not use them to build an advertising profile of a child.

Deleting your data

Delete an alarm or clear its history in the app, or uninstall Alalarm to remove everything it saved on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
